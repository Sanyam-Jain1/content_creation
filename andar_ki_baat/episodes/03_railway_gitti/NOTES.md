# EP03: Railway track ke neeche patthar kyun hote hain?

**Hook:** a train rolls over the stones → "Train ki patri ke neeche ye patthar kyun bichhe hote hain? Sajaavat ke liye? Bilkul nahi."

**Beats:** a freight train weighs thousands of tonnes; straight on soil, the track sinks → the layers: rail, sleeper, then the stones = **BALLAST** →
ballast spreads the load over a bigger area, so the ground holds → *twist:* the stones are angular, not round: they interlock and lock the sleeper,
round pebbles would roll away → rain drains through the gaps: no mud, no sinking → bonus: weeds struggle to grow, vibration is damped →
"Agli baar in pattharon ko thank you bolna." → the stones: "Koi baat nahi!"

**Facts:** Track ballast is crushed angular stone (typically granite/basalt) packed under and around the sleepers. Its jobs: transfer and spread
the load to the formation (subgrade), hold sleepers against lateral and longitudinal movement (angular particles interlock), drain water,
resist vegetation, and absorb some vibration and noise. Heavy-haul freight trains in India weigh several thousand tonnes.
