# EP01: UPI se paisa 2 second mein kaise pahunchta hai?

**Hook:** "Chai ke dus rupaye, scan kiya, aur *ting!*" → soundbox: "Dus rupaye prapt hue." → "Do second mein paisa pahunch gaya. Par kaise?"

**Beats:** chai stall scan + soundbox → does the note fly to the shop? NAHI → flow diagram: your app → NPCI (the "traffic police" of UPI) →
your bank checks PIN + balance, −₹10 → NPCI tells the chaiwala's bank +₹10 → soundbox ting, 2 SEC →
*twist:* no note moved, only ledger entries; banks settle among themselves later, several times a day →
2,000 crore+ transactions a month → *tip:* you never need your PIN to **receive** money; "enter PIN to get money" = SCAM →
"Bhaiya, chai thandi ho gayi."

**Facts:** UPI runs on NPCI's switch: the payer's app sends the request to NPCI, which routes it to the payer's bank (PIN + balance check, debit)
and the payee's bank (credit), then confirms back. Interbank settlement happens later in NPCI settlement cycles, several per day.
Volume: about 2,450 crore (24.5 billion) transactions in August 2026 (NPCI data, via news reports).
A UPI PIN is only ever needed to send money or approve a collect request, never to receive.
