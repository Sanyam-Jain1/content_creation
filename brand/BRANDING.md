# Page branding and setup

The page is a **general storytelling page**: fast, animated, hook-first stories told in
parts. GARBO is the launch series; any genre can follow (history, crime, survival,
science, business, myths…), so the brand must not be tied to one theme.

Visual board: `export/brand_board.png`. All assets: `export/`. Regenerate with
`node brand/export.js` (uses the same character rig as the videos).

---

## 1. Name: WAIT, WHAT?

Chosen. The viewer's reaction is the name: it promises a twist without tying the page to
any genre or to the word "stories".

**Handle: `@waitwhattv`** (chosen and registered). Use the same handle on Instagram,
TikTok and YouTube.

## 2. Profile picture (final)

**The surprised TV**: a retro TV with red-tipped antennae whose screen shows the
"wait, what?" reaction eyes (one brow up, tiny pupils), on brand yellow. The TV ties
to the `tv` in the handle; the antennae give it an outline that reads at 32 px.

## 3. Bio

**Instagram** (≤150 characters):

```
🤯 Real events that sound made up
Animated, in parts, no boring bits
⏭️ New one every week 👇
```

- Name field (searchable): `Wait, What? | Unbelievable True Stories`. Instagram search indexes the name field; put the keyword there. The handle stays free of "stories".
- Category: *Digital creator* or *Entertainment website*.
- Link: one link page (Linktree / Beacons) → YouTube, TikTok, "Sources for every file".

**TikTok** (≤80 characters): `Real events that sound made up. Animated, in parts. New one weekly ⏭️`

**YouTube** channel description:
> Real events that sound made up, told as fast animated series in parts.
> Every episode is based on real events; dialogue is dramatised. Sources are in
> each video's description. New one every week.

## 4. Profile setup

- **Story highlights** (covers in `export/highlight_*.png`): `GARBO` (one highlight per series) · `SOURCES` (the fact sheet: real vs dramatised) · `NEXT` (polls on which story is next) · `ABOUT`.
- **Pinned posts** (pin 3): Part 1 (the hook), the best-performing part, and a 30 s "who was Garbo" trailer cut from Parts 1–4.
- **Grid**: every reel gets a custom cover (template: `export/cover_part1.png`). Rules: one face, a 2–3 word headline in Montserrat Black, the series name + part. Keep everything inside the central 3:4 area (1080×1440) so the grid crop never cuts it.
- **Series naming**: `GARBO · Part 1/4`. Once the name is final, the in-video badge can carry it too (for example `WAIT, WHAT? · GARBO 1/4`), and the reaction eyes can blink as a 0.5 s sting on the last frame.

## 5. Captions, hashtags, posting

Caption template (first line is the hook, because only ~125 characters show before "more"):

```
He fooled Hitler with spies that didn't exist. 🕵️ (Part 1/4)

Juan Pujol was a chicken farmer Britain rejected three times…
so he became a "Nazi spy" and invented an entire army.

GARBO · Part 1/4. Follow for Part 2.
Inspired by true events. Dialogue is dramatised. Sources: link in bio.

#history #ww2 #spy #truestory #dday #animation #historytok #espionage
```

- **Hashtags**: 5–8, mixing broad (`#history #ww2 #truestory`) with niche (`#espionage #dday #doubleagent #garbo`). Same set on TikTok; YouTube Shorts gets `#shorts` plus 2–3 topic tags in the title.
- **Cadence for the launch**: Part 1 → Part 2 two days later → Part 3 two days later → Part 4. Every post 18:00–21:00 in your main audience's time zone. Post the same file to Reels, TikTok and Shorts on the same day (export without platform watermarks).
- **Retention loop**: each part ends with "Follow for Part n"; pin a comment on each part: "Part 2 is up 👉 on the profile".
- **Engagement prompt** in pinned comment: "Would you have fallen for it? 🤔" or "Which story should we tell next?"

## 6. Compliance and rights

- Keep the `INSPIRED BY TRUE EVENTS` / "dialogue is dramatised" line in captions.
- Voices, sound effects and music are generated with ElevenLabs on a paid plan
  (commercial use included). Keep the account on a paid plan while you monetise.
- AI labels: the animation is clearly stylised; the voices are AI-generated. If a platform
  asks whether content is "altered or synthetic", answer according to its current policy
  (YouTube's disclosure covers realistic synthetic content; check the toggle at upload).
- Historical photos used for research are not in the videos; the characters are original drawings.
