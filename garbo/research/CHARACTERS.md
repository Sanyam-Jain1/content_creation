# Character bible: research notes

Visual and vocal reference for every character, built from period photos and
documented descriptions. Art style: **stylised realism**, i.e. flat vector shapes,
but proportions and signature features follow the real people.

Reference photos are **not** committed to this repo (archive/Crown copyright).
Links to each are in *Sources* below.

## Juan Pujol García ("GARBO", German codename "ARABEL")

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Born / died | 14 Feb 1912, Barcelona → 10 Oct 1988, Caracas |
| Ages in the series | 29 (1941) · 31 (1943) · 32 (1944) · 37 (1949) · 72 (1984) |
| Origin & language | Catalan, from Barcelona. Spanish/Catalan native; little English in 1941. |
| Listed profession, 1941 | *Escritor* (writer), on his Brazilian consular card, Lisbon 1941 |

**Look, from the 1941 consular ID photo (Lisbon):**
- Clean-shaven. **No moustache, no glasses** in 1941.
- Strongly **receding hairline**: high bald dome, short dark hair at the sides and back, combed flat.
- Narrow **oval face**, slightly long chin, **prominent ears** set out from the head.
- Straight, fairly long nose; thin lips; a faint, knowing half-smile.
- Dark-brown eyes, heavy-lidded; dark eyebrows, slightly arched.
- Dark double-breasted suit, white shirt, **patterned tie**, white pocket square.

**Look, from the 1931 conscript photo (age 19):** big open grin and the same prominent ears. Use the grin for "performance" moments.

**Look, the famous wartime "disguise" photo:** full dark beard, **round dark-rimmed glasses**, bald dome, pinstripe suit, patterned tie. Use it for the **iconic Garbo poster / thumbnail look** and the 1949 disguise. Not for 1941.

**Look, 1960s Venezuelan passport (profile):** fully bald on top, grey at the sides, light jacket, open collar. Use for Part 4 (bookshop, 1984): add grey hair and age lines.

**Personality:** a born actor (MI5 named him after Greta Garbo for his acting). Charming, quick, amused; quietly emotional underneath.

**Voice:** light-to-mid baritone, quick, warm, Catalan-Spanish accent in English.

## Araceli González Carballo (later "Araceli González de Pujol")

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Born | 5 Jul 1919, **Lugo, Galicia**. Galician, not Catalan. |
| Ages in the series | 21–22 (1941) · 23–24 (1943) · 29–30 (1949) |
| Language | Spanish (Galician background). **Spoke no English** in London. |
| Children | Son Juan Fernando (baby in 1941); three children by 1948 |

**Look, from the 1941 consular ID photo (Lisbon):**
- **Round, full face**, soft jaw, warm wide smile.
- **Dark wavy hair**, shoulder length, centre-left part, 1940s rolled waves.
- Dark eyes, natural brows.
- Dark dress, V-neck, with a **white bow brooch** on the left collar.

**Personality:** sharp and a gifted performer (she once convinced the Germans that Juan was in England while he was in Portugal, and helped invent the spies). In London: homesick, isolated, "too much macaroni, too many potatoes, not enough fish". On 21 June 1943 she threatened to go to the Spanish embassy, and turned on the gas taps in their Hendon kitchen.

**Voice:** young adult female, bright, then fraying. Galician-Spanish accent.

## Tomás "Tommy" Harris (MI5 case officer)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Born / died | 10 Apr 1908, Hampstead → 1964, Mallorca (car crash) |
| Ages in the series | 34–36 |
| Background | Art dealer (father Lionel Harris, Spanish Art Gallery, Mayfair). **Spanish mother**, educated mainly in Spain, fluent in Spanish. Played piano and saxophone. |

**Look:** "striking Mediterranean features… swept-black hair, beaky nose, and intense dark eyes" (Costello). His 1942 MI5 ID photo: long face, heavy dark brows, deep-set eyes, black hair swept back from the forehead, dark suit.

**Personality:** "enchantingly enigmatic", "amazingly generous", "expansive… when in the mood". Dry, amused, unflappable.

**Voice:** educated London English (RP), relaxed and dry. Could slip into fluent Spanish.

## "Frederico" (Friedrich Knappe-Ratey, Abwehr, Madrid)

- Abwehr officer in Madrid who recruited and handled Pujol. (One source names him as Gustav Knittel instead; we use Wikipedia's Knappe-Ratey, and on screen he is only "Frederico".)
- Senior boss above him: Karl-Erich Kühlenthal ("Don Pablo" in Madrid cafés).
- **No reliable photo found.** Design an archetype: 1940s German officer in civilian clothes, about 40, fair hair cut short at the sides and side-parted, small round steel glasses, firm jaw, dark three-piece suit and party pin. Proud, gullible, eager.

**Voice:** German accent in English; native German for the `[DE]` line.

## Nigel West (Rupert Allason)

- Born 8 Nov 1951, London. Age 32 in 1984.
- **No photo checked.** Design as a 1980s British writer: dark side-parted hair, clean-shaven, tweed jacket, open collar.
- **Voice:** RP English, crisp.
