# EP03: What if you shrank 10× smaller?

Sibling to EP00 (10× stronger). Post them a few days apart and pin each in the other's comments.

**Hook:** "Tonight, you shrink to one tenth of your size. You wake up 17 centimetres tall." →
"Your cat is now bigger than a horse. And it's looking at you like a snack."

**Beats:** shrink on the pillow, 17 CM ruler → looming cat vs. horse (YUM) → good news: indestructible, jump off a skyscraper, NOT A SCRATCH →
1 cookie = 5 days → record scratch: square-cube law, LOSES HEAT 10× FASTER → freezing in a sock at 30 °C →
*real pitched-up line:* "Tiny vocal cords. You now sound like this!" → unlocking the phone = full-body workout →
sock-drawer bunker ✓ WARM ✓ SOFT ✓ CAT-PROOF → "Mostly cat-proof. The cat has been waiting outside for three days." (giant eye in the gap)

**Science:** 1/10 height = 1/1000 mass (about 70 g); surface-to-volume ratio ×10, so heat loss outpaces heat production
(square-cube law). Kleiber's law gives ≈ 11 kcal/day, so one 53 kcal cookie ≈ 5 days. A mouse-sized body survives huge falls
(Haldane, *On Being the Right Size*). Smaller vocal folds give a higher voice.
