# EP02: What if you could eat sunlight?

**Hook:** "Tomorrow morning, you wake up green. Your skin can photosynthesize, just like a plant." →
fantasy ("Lunch is just… standing outside") → reversal: "Sounds perfect. Until you do the math."

**Beats:** green in the mirror (sprout pops) → green crowd on lunch break → chalkboard maths, calculator ERROR →
your skin ≈ one door (1.8 m²) → time-lapse sunbathing, energy battery reaches 1 % → "two crackers" →
zoom out: you'd need a tennis court of skin (≈ 200 m²) → "tiny human, giant flappy skin cape" (blown away) →
one cloudy week, battery 0 % → a jogging tree collapses: "That's exactly why trees don't go jogging. They literally can't afford it."

**Science:** skin ≈ 1 % of daily needs; a tennis court of skin needed (Live Science, plant ecologist Lindsay Turnbull);
about 290 h of peak sun per day's food (Naked Scientists). 1 % of 2,000 kcal ≈ 20 kcal ≈ two crackers.
