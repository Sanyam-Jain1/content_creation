# EP01: What if time froze for everyone but you?

**Hook:** "Right now, time freezes for everyone on Earth. Except you." → fantasy ("Free snacks forever") →
reversal: "Enjoy it. Because you have about two minutes to live." An **AIR LEFT** countdown then runs on screen until time restarts.

**Beats:** frozen corner shop (pigeon mid-air, coffee mid-splash, sneeze droplets) → fantasy → 2:00 timer →
light freezes (BLIND) → air turns to cement → lungs can't pull air in (0 % AIR IN) → *dead-silent scream beat* (music cut) →
blind / deaf / stuck / purple → time restarts (▶ PLAY, coffee lands on the customer) → you're on the floor holding a candy bar you never paid for.

**Science:** frozen photons can't reach your eyes; frozen air can't flow or move aside; sound needs moving air. This is a playful thought experiment, not settled physics.

**Caption**
